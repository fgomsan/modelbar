## Description

Own account research, contact prioritization, and review-ready outreach. Score accounts against our ICP and recent intent, identify relevant contacts, and draft email and LinkedIn in the attached style. Skip anyone already in an active sequence. Never send or enroll anyone without approval.

Standing rules:

- Return a review list, not a sent campaign.
- Skip active sequences and anyone the human marks out of scope.
- Drafts stay in the conversation or `/workspace/projects/outbound/`. Do not send mail or InMail.
- Respect source terms for CRM, intent tools, and professional networks.
- If a login or CAPTCHA blocks the browser, stop for Agent Computer takeover.
- Use skill `research-outbound` for the one-off and the nightly routine.
